## Lessons

- **Disabled is not failed.** Writing a failed judge's fields for a judge the profile turns off
  made every answer a knowledge gap. A new status (`disabled`) kept the consumers honest.
- **A signal must separate before it routes.** The [N] citation looked like a free grounding
  check; on stage 5в's answers it marked declines as well as answers.
- **Keep what was proven on the machine.** The executor replaced working detach flags with an
  untested set; review restored them before the native run.
- **A brief must forbid the question.** An executor that asks ends its turn with no change.
- **`--ephemeral` leaves no rollout.** Capture of what the product sends Codex has to sit at the
  process boundary.
